Develops a neural network-based scheme for detecting sensor and actuator attacks in nonlinear networked control systems. The approach simultaneously detects attack presence and approximates the attack signal to enable mitigation. Published in IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems, with 61+ citations.
