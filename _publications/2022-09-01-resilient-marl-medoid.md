Introduces medoid and soft-medoid aggregation rules to achieve Byzantine resilience in multi-agent reinforcement learning. Unlike mean-based aggregation, medoid-based methods are robust to a bounded fraction of adversarial agents sending poisoned gradient updates. Provides convergence guarantees under Byzantine faults. Presented at IEEE ICAA 2022.
