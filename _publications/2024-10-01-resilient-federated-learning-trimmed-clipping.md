Presents trimmed-clipping aggregation, a new robust aggregation rule for federated learning that combines gradient trimming with norm clipping to defend against Byzantine and model poisoning attacks. The method provides provable resilience guarantees while maintaining high accuracy on benign data. Presented at IEEE TPS-ISA 2024. Student travel award recipient.
