Proposes a modified χ² (chi-squared) detector for cyber-attack detection and mitigation in nonlinear stochastic systems. The modified detector is designed to handle nonlinear dynamics while providing statistical guarantees on false alarm and detection rates. Presented at IEEE CDC 2019.
