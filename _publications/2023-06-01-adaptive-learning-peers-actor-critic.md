Proposes an adaptive aggregation scheme for distributed actor-critic reinforcement learning, where each agent selectively weights updates received from peers based on gradient similarity and historical performance. The method improves convergence speed and resilience compared to uniform aggregation baselines. Presented at DCAI 2023.
