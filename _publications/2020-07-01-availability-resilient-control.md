Presents a control design methodology for uncertain linear stochastic networked control systems that achieves resilience to denial-of-service (DoS) attacks affecting communication channel availability. Guarantees closed-loop stability and performance bounds despite intermittent packet loss induced by attacks. Presented at ACC 2020.
