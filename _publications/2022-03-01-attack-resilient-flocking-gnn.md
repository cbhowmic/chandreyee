Uses graph neural networks (GNNs) to design attack-resilient flocking controllers for multi-agent systems. The GNN-based controller learns to identify and discount the influence of adversarial agents, maintaining flocking cohesion and collision avoidance even when a fraction of agents send malicious control signals. Presented at MED 2022.
