Book chapter presenting a learning-based methodology for detecting and estimating cyber-attacks in cyber-physical systems. The approach uses neural networks to approximate unknown attack signals while maintaining system stability. Published in Artificial Neural Networks for Engineering Applications (Academic Press/Elsevier).
