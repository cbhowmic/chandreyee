Multi-agent formation control framework that simultaneously achieves trajectory tracking, inter-agent collision avoidance, and network connectivity maintenance. Uses a consensus-based estimated flocking center approach for distributed coordination among agents with a leader-follower architecture. Published in IEEE Systems Journal, with 106+ citations.
