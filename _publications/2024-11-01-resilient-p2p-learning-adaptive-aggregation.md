Develops an adaptive aggregation scheme for resilient peer-to-peer (serverless) federated learning. Each node adaptively weights model updates from neighbors based on similarity metrics, achieving Byzantine resilience without a trusted central aggregator. **Received the Best Paper Award at IEEE DCAI 2024.**
