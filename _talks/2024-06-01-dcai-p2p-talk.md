Presented an adaptive aggregation scheme for fully decentralized (serverless) peer-to-peer federated learning. Each node adaptively weights model updates from its network neighbors based on gradient similarity, achieving Byzantine resilience without a central coordinator. This work received the **Best Paper Award** at IEEE DCAI 2024.
