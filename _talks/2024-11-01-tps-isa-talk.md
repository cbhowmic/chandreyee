Presented novel trimmed-clipping aggregation for federated learning that provides provable resilience against Byzantine and model poisoning attacks. Recipient of the **Student Travel Award** to attend this conference. The talk covered theoretical convergence guarantees and empirical evaluation against state-of-the-art poisoning attacks on MNIST, CIFAR-10, and other benchmarks.
