Served as the primary **Instructor** for EE3321 Control Systems Laboratory at Missouri University of Science & Technology from January 2019 to December 2019. Responsibilities included designing and delivering laboratory sessions on classical and modern control systems, supervising undergraduate students in hands-on experiments with MATLAB/Simulink, grading lab reports, and conducting office hours. Course topics included PID control, stability analysis, root locus, and frequency domain methods.
