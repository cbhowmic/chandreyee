Served as **Teaching Assistant** for two graduate-level courses at IIT Kanpur from July 2015 to May 2016:

- **EE671A Neural Networks** — Assisted with tutorials and assignments on feedforward networks, backpropagation, recurrent networks, and applications in pattern recognition and control.
- **EE653A Digital Control** — Assisted with problem sessions on discrete-time system modeling, z-transform analysis, digital PID design, and state-space methods for sampled-data systems.

Responsibilities included holding tutorial sessions, grading assignments and exams, and providing student support during office hours.
